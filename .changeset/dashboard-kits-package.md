Add `@openuidev/dashboard-kits`: e-commerce and product analytics dashboard kits — components, data-tool contracts, prompts, and sample data a model composes into dashboards. Its charts (TrendChart, ColumnChart, HeatGrid, ShareBar, GoalPace, Tracker, Sparkline, ProfitWaterfall, SegmentFlow, QuadrantChart, SixTwelve, BurnDown) are drawn on d3 with one shared interaction model and follow react-ui's chart architecture.
