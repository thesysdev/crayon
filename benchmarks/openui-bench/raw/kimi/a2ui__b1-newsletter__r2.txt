Both requirements are covered:
1. **What subscribers get** — the `TextContent` block delivers a single sentence describing the weekly roundup (launches, teardowns, growth tactics, under five minutes to read).
2. **Email entry + subscribe** — the `Form` contains an email-typed `Input` with validation-friendly labeling, plus a primary **Subscribe** button.
