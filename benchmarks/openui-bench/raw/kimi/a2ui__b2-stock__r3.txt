How each requirement is covered:
1. **Low items + remaining units** — the table lists each item with its SKU and exact units remaining (sorted worst-first).
2. **Reorder per item** — the rightmost action column gives every row its own "Reorder" action.
3. **Threshold** — the warning alert states the trigger clearly: stock at or below 10 units.
4. **Search** — a search field (by name or SKU) with Search/Clear buttons filters the list.
