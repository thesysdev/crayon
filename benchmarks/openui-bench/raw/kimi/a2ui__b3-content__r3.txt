How the eight items map to the screen:

1. **Drafts with status** — the *Drafts* table lists each piece with a Status column (In review, Needs edits, Ready, Draft) and last-updated time.
2. **Published this week** — a stat card showing "12 pieces live" with a weekly comparison.
3. **Most-read this month** — a stat card highlighting "The Quiet Rebuild of Downtown" with reads, read time, and a category tag.
4. **Readership, past 30 days** — a natural line chart of daily page views.
5. **Narrow by category** — a category Select (All, News, Features, Opinion, Reviews) in the top controls.
6. **Schedule a piece** — a form with a title input and a single-date DatePicker plus a Schedule button.
7. **Comments awaiting reply** — a numbered list with commenter, excerpt, source article, age, and a Reply action.
8. **Start a new draft** — the primary "＋ Start new draft" button in the top-right.
