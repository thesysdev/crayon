This single-screen job posting covers all six requirements:

1. **Role title & team** — displayed prominently in the card header with tags for employment type, level, and location.
2. **Role description** — a concise paragraph summarizing the position and its impact.
3. **Qualifications** — a numbered list of five key requirements with supporting detail.
4. **Salary range** — clearly stated with context on what influences the final offer.
5. **Application form** — collects full name, email, and a resume/portfolio link with validation rules and a submit button.
6. **Share options** — buttons for LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Email, and Copy Link to distribute the posting.
