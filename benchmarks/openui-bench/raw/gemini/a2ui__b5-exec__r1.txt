An executive command dashboard tailored for a CEO's quarterly view.

It incorporates all 18 required elements into a high-density, top-level executive overview layout:
- **Filters & Controls**: Quarter selection (Q3 2024 active), Regional drill-down (Global/NA/EMEA/APAC), and dynamic Export Pack actions.
- **Top Financial & Operational Metrics**: Key metrics including Months of Runway, Pipeline, Win Rate, Customer Count & Churn, and NPS.
- **Financial Performance**: Core financials (Revenue, Costs, Net Profit) alongside multi-quarter trends across Q4-23 to Q3-24.
- **Departmental Budget & Headcount**: Breakdown of Departmental Budgets vs Plan alongside Headcount distribution.
- **Strategy & Governance**: Active Strategic Initiatives with status badges, Top Enterprise Risks with assigned C-level owners, and standard Board Meeting agenda items.
