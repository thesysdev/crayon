Here is a complete UI for an Editor's Content Studio designed to address all 8 specified requirements in a single interface:

1. **Drafts with status** (in the Content Overview section & quick actions)
2. **Pieces published this week** (detailed in the recent items list)
3. **Most-read article this month** (featured key metric callout/card)
4. **Readership over the past 30 days** (30-day traffic trend chart)
5. **Category filter** (interactive selector at the top)
6. **Schedule a piece** (embedded form with Title & Publish Date)
7. **Comments awaiting reply** (moderation table/list)
8. **Start a new draft** (prominent quick action button)
