An interactive, comprehensive dashboard tailored for a PMO (Project Management Office) Portfolio Review.

It covers all requested items:
1. **Quarter Selector & Actions:** Quarter dropdown (Q1-Q4 2025/2024), "Request Resources", "Export Review", "Archive Project".
2. **Portfolio Summary:** One-line executive summary banner with key health indicators.
3. **Tabs for Structured Drill-down:**
   - **Overview & Performance:** Sprints delivery pace trend line chart and team utilization progress breakdown.
   - **Projects & Budgets:** Project inventory with health status, owners, deadlines, budget vs allocation breakdown, and direct archive actions.
   - **Milestones & Deadlines:** Overdue milestones list and upcoming 2-week deadlines.
   - **Risks & Blockers:** High/Medium severity risk matrix and currently blocked work items.
4. **Interactive Action Dialogs:** Modals to handle resource requests, review export, and project archiving.
