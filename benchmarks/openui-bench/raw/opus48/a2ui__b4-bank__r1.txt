This single screen covers every item:

1. **Balances** — checking, savings, and credit shown in the top row.
2. **Recent transactions** — a table with date, merchant, and amount.
3. **Spending split** — a donut chart of category spend this month (plus a trend line).
4. **Bills due soon** — a table with bill, due date, and amount.
5. **Vacation goal** — a progress bar at 64% toward $5,000.
6. **Credit score** — 742 with a "Very Good" badge.
7. **Move money** — the "Move Money" tab with from/to accounts and an amount field.
8. **Freeze credit line** — a confirm dialog triggered by the destructive button.
9. **Statement period** — the "Statements" tab with a date-range picker.
10. **Manage alerts** — the "Alerts" tab with toggles for each trigger.
11. **Dispute a transaction** — a dialog form with transaction, reason, and details.
