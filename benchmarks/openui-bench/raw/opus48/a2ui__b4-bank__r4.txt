1. **Balances** — Checking, Savings and Credit cards at the top.
2. **Recent transactions** — table with merchant, date and amount.
3. **Spending split** — donut chart in the "Spending" tab.
4. **Bills due soon** — table in the "Bills Due" tab.
5. **Vacation goal** — progress bar in the "Vacation Goal" tab.
6. **Credit score** — large 742 score in the "Credit Score" tab.
7. **Move money** — transfer form with from/to accounts and an amount field.
8. **Freeze credit line** — confirm dialog button.
9. **Statement period** — range date picker.
10. **Manage alerts** — switch group of alert triggers.
11. **Dispute a transaction** — dialog with transaction selector, reason and details.
