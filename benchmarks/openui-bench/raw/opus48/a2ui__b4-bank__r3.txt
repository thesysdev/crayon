4. **Bills due soon** — a numbered list with due dates and amounts.
5. **Vacation savings goal** — a progress bar at 68%.
6. **Credit score** — a heading plus progress bar (742 / 850).
7. **Move money** — a transfer form with from/to accounts and an amount field.
8. **Freeze the credit line** — a confirm dialog in the Security tab.
9. **Pick a statement period** — a select in the Statements tab.
10. **Manage alert triggers** — toggles in the Alerts tab.
11. **Dispute a transaction** — a full form in the Dispute tab.
