6. **Deadlines next two weeks** — dedicated upcoming list
7. **Delivery pace** — line chart across the last six sprints
8. **Blocked work items** — dedicated list with blocker cause and age
9. **One-line health summary** — the portfolio alert banner at the top
10. **View a different quarter** — quarter selector in the controls bar
11. **Request more people** — primary action button
12. **Export the review** — export button in the controls bar
13. **Archive a finished project** — tertiary action button
