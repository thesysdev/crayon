1. **Drafts with status** — the Drafts table shows title, category, status, and last-updated for each.
2. **Published this week** — the "Published This Week" metric card (12, +3 vs last week).
3. **Most-read article this month** — a dedicated card highlighting the top performer with reads and shares.
4. **Readership over 30 days** — an area chart of daily unique readers.
5. **Narrow by category** — the category filter dropdown near the top.
6. **Schedule a piece** — a form with a title input and a publish-date picker.
7. **Comments awaiting reply** — a numbered list with reply actions plus an at-a-glance metric.
8. **Start a new draft** — the primary "Start New Draft" button at the top.
