1. **Items running low with remaining units** — the inventory table lists each flagged item with its "Units Remaining" column (and status), plus per-item unit counts repeated in the reorder list.
2. **A way to reorder each item** — the reorder section provides a per-item "Reorder" action for every low-stock product.
3. **The threshold that triggers the warning** — shown in the top alert ("Reorder threshold: 20 units") and as a dedicated "Threshold" column in the table.
4. **A way to search items** — the search input filters by product name or SKU at the top of the view.
