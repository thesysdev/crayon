I have created a personal budget dashboard screen for you. It includes:

1. **Total Spent vs. Budget**: A visual spending summary featuring an overall progress bar and indicator badges.
2. **Category Split**: A horizontal bar chart breaking down expenses by category (Housing, Food, Transport, Utilities, Entertainment).
3. **Largest Expense Highlight**: An explicit warning/highlight block calling out the single highest expense of the month (Rent Payment).
4. **Month Selector & Add Expense Action**: A month selector input to switch viewing periods, as well as an "Add Expense" dialog form to log new purchases on the fly.
