I have created a comprehensive analytics dashboard for your training platform.

It includes:
- **Cohort Selector**: Quick dropdown to filter metrics by specific cohorts (e.g., Q1 Engineering Cohort, Sales Onboarding).
- **Key Metric Indicators**: At-a-glance KPI summary for active learners, completion rates, and average quiz scores.
- **Engagement Trend**: An 8-week area chart tracking active learner trends over time.
- **Top Courses & Risk Management**: Side-by-side view showing courses ranked by enrollment and a table of learners at risk of dropping out.
- **Action Center**: A direct tool to compose and send messages to selected cohorts.
