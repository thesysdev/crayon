I have created a personal budget check interface that covers all your requested items:

1. **Total Spent vs. Budget**: Prominently displayed using a progress bar along with key metrics showing total spent, total budget, and remaining funds.
2. **Category Split**: A visual donut chart breakdown along with detailed breakdown statistics for housing, groceries, dining, transport, and utilities.
3. **Largest Expense Highlight**: A dedicated alert banner pinpointing the top expense (Rent Payment) with date and category context.
4. **Month Switcher**: A month selector dropdown allowing quick navigation between different monthly statements.
5. **Add Expense Form**: An embedded interactive form with inputs for expense description, amount, category selection, and date picker.
