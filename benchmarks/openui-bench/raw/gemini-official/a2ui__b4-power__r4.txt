### Features Covered:
1. **Current Electricity Load**: Highlighted in the top summary card (`38,450 MW`).
2. **Capacity Headroom**: Progress indicator displaying `85.4%` utilization against the 45.0 GW maximum threshold.
3. **Generation Split by Source**: Donut chart detailing Wind/Solar, Nuclear, Gas, Hydro, Storage, and Coal/Biomass.
4. **Plant Output & Status**: Detailed interactive table displaying every plant's real-time megawatt output, total capacity, and status.
5. **Outages with Affected Region**: Active grid outages table with incident cause, affected customer count, and ETA.
6. **24-Hour Demand Forecast**: Natural line chart comparing predicted load against grid capacity over time.
7. **Current Price**: Real-time electricity spot price display ($64.20 / MWh).
8. **Planned Maintenance**: Dedicated tab displaying upcoming monthly equipment servicing, windows, and MW capacity impacts.
9. **Live Alerts**: Alarm feed displaying critical, warning, and informational SCADA events with quick actions.
10. **Regional Focus Control**: Header selector enabling operators to filter grid telemetry by specific regional zones.
11. **Order Curtailment Action**: Form to select target region, curtailment volume (MW), protocol type, and submit dispatch commands.
12. **Emissions Today**: Live tally card tracking carbon emissions (142,800 tCO₂e) and current carbon intensity.
