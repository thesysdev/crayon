## Requirement Coverage

| Requirement | Where it appears on the screen |
|---|---|
| **1. Today’s appointments with times and patient names** | Main appointment list shows time, patient name, provider, room, and status. |
| **2. How many patients are expected** | Day Summary shows **“Patients expected today: 32.”** |
| **3. No-show rate this month** | Day Summary shows **“No-show rate this month: 7.4%.”** |
| **4. Which rooms are free right now** | Right panel shows **“Rooms Free Right Now”** with live room status. |
| **5. The next patient called out** | Right panel shows **“Next Patient Called Out”** with patient name, appointment time, provider, room, and actions. |
| **6. A way to check a patient in** | Each appointment row has a **Check In** action, and the bottom **Quick Check-In** panel lets the receptionist confirm details, assign a room, and check the patient in. |
| **7. A way to move to another day** | Top navigation includes **Previous**, **Today**, **Date Picker**, and **Next**. |

---

## Recommended Interaction Rules

- **Check In** should only be enabled for appointments with status **Scheduled**, **Called out**, or **Arrived**.
- After check-in, the appointment status changes to **Checked in** and the assigned room updates in the room panel.
- If a room is selected during check-in, the room panel immediately changes that room from **FREE** to **Busy**.
- The **Next Patient Called Out** card should always show the earliest patient who has been called but is not yet checked in.
- Changing the date using the date picker refreshes the appointment list, expected patient count, and room availability for that day.
