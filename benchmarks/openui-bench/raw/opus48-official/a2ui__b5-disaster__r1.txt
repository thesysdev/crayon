This single-screen dashboard covers all 18 requirements:

1. **Active incidents by zone** — table with zone, incident type, severity, and status
2. **Overall severity summary** — count tags plus a donut chart breakdown
3. **Resources** — tabbed vehicles / personnel / supplies views
4. **Shelters** — capacity, occupancy, and free spaces per shelter
5. **Evacuation route status** — open/congested/closed per route
6. **48-hour weather** — line chart (rainfall/wind) plus a condition/alert table
7. **Rescued & casualties** — headline metric cards (plus missing)
8. **Volunteers** — name, assignment, and zone
9. **Donations** — bar chart and itemized table
10. **Aid requests** — pending requests with priority
11. **Communication channels** — up/degraded/down status
12. **Roads closed** — road and closure reason
13. **Nearby hospitals** — free beds and free ICU beds
14. **Open tasks** — task, assignee, and status
15. **Request resources** — form with type, quantity, and destination
16. **Broadcast update** — channel selection and message
17. **Focus zone** — radio selection to center on a zone
18. **File situation report** — title, severity, and detailed body

The four action forms are grouped into tabs at the bottom for quick coordinator access.
