How each requirement is covered:

1. **Followers + growth** — big number card with 30-day delta and a trend line chart
2. **Engagement rate** — 4.8% KPI card with progress toward a stretch goal
3. **Best posts** — ranked table showing each post, its platform, and its reach
4. **Audience split** — donut chart across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X
5. **Scheduled this week** — numbered list of 5 queued posts with day/time/platform
6. **Best hour to post** — dedicated card (7–8 PM weekdays, from 90 days of history)
7. **Unreplied comments** — "23 waiting" badge plus the newest comments with Reply actions
8. **Earnings this month** — $2,340 KPI card with month-over-month change
9. **Collab requests** — "3 pending" badge with brand deals, values, and Review actions
10. **Post scheduler** — form with caption, platform, date, and time fields
11. **Platform filter** — the dropdown at the top narrows the whole screen to one platform
