3. **Best sellers** — table with units + revenue
4. **Out of stock** — table with SKU and time out
5. **On shift today** — staff table with roles, times, status
6. **Avg basket size** — KPI card with transaction context
7. **Returns this week** — KPI card with count, value, and top reason
8. **Promotions** — table with redemptions, revenue, status
9. **Registers** — table showing open/closed/maintenance
10. **Reorder** — embedded form (item select + quantity + submit)
11. **View another store** — store selector in the header
12. **Week comparison** — grouped bar chart, this week vs last week
