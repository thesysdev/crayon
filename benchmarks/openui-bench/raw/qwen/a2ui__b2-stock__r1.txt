This screen covers all four requirements:

| # | Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Items running low + remaining units | Table with **Item Name**, **SKU**, and **Remaining Units** columns |
| 2 | Way to reorder each item | **Action column** with a "Reorder" button per row |
| 3 | Threshold that triggers the warning | **Warning alert banner** at the top stating the 10-unit threshold |
| 4 | Way to search items | **Search input** with placeholder and hint text for filtering |

The view also includes a status indicator (🔴 Critical / 🟡 Low) and a summary footer showing the total count of flagged items and how many are critically low.
