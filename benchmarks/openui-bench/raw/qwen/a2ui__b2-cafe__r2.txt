Notes on the design:

- **Special call-out**: The `Callout` banner sits directly under the header so it's the first thing customers see. The combo pricing is consistent with the menu items ($4.50 latte + $3.75 muffin → $6.50, save $1.75).
- **Vegetarian filter**: The tab switcher gives an instant, no-input way to see vegetarian-only items — the vegetarian tab excludes the Bone Broth, Ham Toastie, Bacon Roll, and Chicken Wrap, and shows a confirmation note. 🌱 badges in the full menu make it easy to spot vegetarian items without switching tabs.
- **Extensible**: Prices and hours live in simple column data arrays, so updating the menu later is just a data change.
