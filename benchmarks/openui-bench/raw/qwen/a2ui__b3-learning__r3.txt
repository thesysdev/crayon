**Coverage checklist:**
1. **Active learners this week** — KPI card showing 1,247 with weekly trend
2. **Course completion rate** — KPI card (84%, down 2 pts)
3. **Average quiz score** — KPI card (88%, up 3 pts)
4. **Courses ranked by enrollment** — horizontal bar chart, top course first
5. **Learners at risk** — warning alert plus a table with cohort, risk level, last-active date, quiz average, and a per-row "Message" action
6. **Narrow to one cohort** — cohort Select + Apply button at the top of the screen
7. **Engagement across 8 weeks** — two-series line chart (active learners + quiz submissions)
8. **Message a cohort** — compose form with recipient cohort selector, message body, and send button; the success callout confirms delivery

Since the form actions are wired, submitting the message form will send the form data (selected cohort + message) back to your agent, where you can handle the actual delivery and update the confirmation text accordingly.
