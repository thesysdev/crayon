1. **Total spent vs. budget** — summary card shows $2,845 spent of $3,500, with a progress bar at 81% and the remaining amount.
2. **Spending split by category** — donut chart with a small-text legend breaking down dollar amounts per category.
3. **Largest expense highlighted** — warning callout flagging Rent at $1,200 and its share of total spending.
4. **Different month** — month selector at the top of the summary card (defaults to October 2025).
5. **Add an expense** — form with description, amount, category, and date fields plus a primary "Add expense" button.
