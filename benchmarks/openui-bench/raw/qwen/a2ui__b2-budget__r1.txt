Here's how each requirement is covered:

| # | Requirement | Component Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total spent vs. budget | **Progress** bar (78%) + **TextContent** with dollar figures |
| 2 | Spending split by category | **PieChart** (donut) with 6 categories |
| 3 | Largest expense highlighted | **Alert** (warning variant) calling out Rent at $950 |
| 4 | View a different month | **Select** dropdown with months Jan–Jul 2025 |
| 5 | Add an expense | **DialogBlock** that opens a modal **Form** with amount, category, description, and date fields |
