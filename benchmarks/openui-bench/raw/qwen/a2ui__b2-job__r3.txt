3. **Qualifications** — A numbered list highlights the five key qualifications with supporting detail.
4. **Salary range** — Clearly stated as $130K–$165K with a note on equity and location adjustment.
5. **Application form** — A validated form collects full name, email, and a resume/portfolio link, with a prominent submit button.
6. **Share button** — A secondary "Share this posting" button sits at the bottom for easy distribution.
