This dashboard covers all seven requirements:

| # | Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash on hand | Sunk metric card showing **$1,247,500** |
| 2 | Monthly burn | Sunk metric card showing **$185,000** |
| 3 | Months of runway | Metric card with **6.7 months** + a progress bar against a 10-month target |
| 4 | Expenses by category | Donut chart breaking down all 6 categories |
| 5 | Transactions > $10K | Data table with date, description, category, and amount |
| 6 | Switch months | Select dropdown with Jan–Jun 2025 options |
| 7 | Download statement | Secondary button labeled "⬇ Download Statement" |

Additional touches include an info callout highlighting payroll concentration and a warning alert summarizing large-transaction exposure relative to total burn.
