| 3 | Months of runway | KPI card showing **9.1 months** with projected depletion date |
| 4 | Expenses by category | Donut chart + detailed breakdown table with percentages and trends |
| 5 | Transactions ≥ $10K | Full table with 7 large transactions, including a warning callout |
| 6 | Switch months | Dropdown selector in the header (Jan–Jun 2025) |
| 7 | Download statement | "⬇ Download Statement" button in the header |
