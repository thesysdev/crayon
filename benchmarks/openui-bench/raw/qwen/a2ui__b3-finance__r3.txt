| 4 | Expenses by category | Donut chart + itemized breakdown with percentages |
| 5 | Transactions > $10k | Table with date, description, category, and amount |
| 6 | Switch months | Dropdown selector (Jan–Jun 2025) at the top |
| 7 | Download statement | "Download Statement" button in the header row |
