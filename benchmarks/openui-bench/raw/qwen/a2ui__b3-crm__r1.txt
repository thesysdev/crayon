**Coverage of your nine items:**

1. **Account name & owner** — "Northwind Logistics" header with owner line; owner shown as "No owner set" since none was provided.
2. **Total deal value** — `$128,400` stat card.
3. **Current deal stage** — "Proposal" tag with a stage 3-of-5 progress bar.
4. **Recent activity in order** — numbered list, most recent first.
5. **Contacts with roles** — 4 contacts (Buyer, Technical Evaluator, Legal, Champion).
6. **Open follow-ups with due dates** — table with task, due date, and status.
7. **Jot a note** — top-left form with a textarea and **Save Note** button.
8. **Renewal date + near-renewal warning** — red "Renews in 19 days" banner and alert text (renewal < 30 days triggers the warning).
9. **Log a call** — form with contact picker, summary, outcome, date, and duration, plus **Log Call** button.

If you'd like the missing owner filled in or the warning threshold adjusted (e.g., < 14 or < 60 days), just say the word.
