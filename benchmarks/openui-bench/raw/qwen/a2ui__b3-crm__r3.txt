This single screen covers all nine requirements:

| # | Requirement | Component Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account name & owner | `CardHeader` |
| 2 | Total deal value | KPI table inside a sunk card |
| 3 | Current deal stage | `Badge` + `Progress` bar |
| 4 | Recent activity (ordered) | `Table` sorted by date |
| 5 | Contacts with roles | `Table` with role column |
| 6 | Open follow-ups + due dates | `Table` with priority |
| 7 | Jot a note | Tab with `TextArea` form |
| 8 | Renewal date + warning | `Alert` (warning variant) |
| 9 | Log a call | Tab with full call-logging form |

The two action forms (note & call log) are tucked into **tabs** at the bottom to keep the page scannable while keeping everything on one screen.
