1. **Where the package is right now** — A step-by-step progress tracker shows the delivery journey, with the current stop highlighted ("In transit — Memphis, TN hub"), plus a text summary of the current location.

2. **Estimated delivery date** — Displayed prominently in bold: "Monday, June 16 between 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM."

3. **Report a problem** — A "Report a Problem" button opens a dialog with a form where the customer can select the issue type (late, damaged, wrong item, missing, or other) and provide a description.
