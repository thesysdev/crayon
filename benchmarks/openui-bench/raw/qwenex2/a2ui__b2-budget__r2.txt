| 2 | Spending split by category | **PieChart** (donut) with 5 categories |
| 3 | Largest expense highlighted | **Alert** (warning) calling out Rent at $1,200 |
| 4 | Switch to a different month | **Select** dropdown at the top with month options |
| 5 | Add an expense | **DialogBlock** that opens a modal form with description, amount, category, and date fields |
