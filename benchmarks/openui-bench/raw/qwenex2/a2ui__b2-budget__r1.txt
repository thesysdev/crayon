

Here's a single-screen personal budget check. Notes on the layout:
- The **progress bar** is right under the month selector so the budget health is the first thing you see after picking a month.
- The **donut chart** gives the visual split, and the **table** underneath adds exact amounts and share percentages.
- The **warning alert** singles out the largest expense with a short, actionable note.
- Swapping the sample figures for real data just means updating the progress label/value, the chart values, and the table columns.
