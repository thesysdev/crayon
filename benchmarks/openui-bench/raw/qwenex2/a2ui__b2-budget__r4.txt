| # | Requirement | Component Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total spent vs. budget | **Progress bar** — shows $2,340 / $3,000 (78%) |
| 2 | Spending by category | **Donut PieChart** — 5 categories with dollar values |
| 3 | Largest expense highlighted | **Warning Alert** — calls out Rent at $950 (41% of spend) |
| 4 | Switch months | **Select dropdown** — Jan–Jul 2025 |
| 5 | Add an expense | **DialogBlock** — opens a modal form with description, amount, category, and date fields |
