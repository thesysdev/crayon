## Required sections covered

### 1. Current headcount
Show as a top-line KPI card.

**Component:** KPI card  
**Title:** Current Headcount  
**Example value:** `248`  
**Optional supporting text:** `+6 this month`

---

### 2. Open roles with department for each
Show as a compact table on the main screen.

**Component:** Table  
**Columns:**
- Role
- Department
- Status
- Days open
- Hiring manager

**Example rows:**

| Role | Department | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Product Manager | Product | Active |
| Data Analyst | Analytics | Recruiting |
| Frontend Engineer | Engineering | Interviewing |
| Customer Success Lead | Customer Success | New |
| Talent Partner | People | Active |

Every open role must clearly display its associated department.

---

### 3. Candidates at each pipeline stage
Show as a horizontal funnel or bar chart.

**Component:** Pipeline funnel / horizontal bar chart  
**Stages:**
- Applied
- Recruiter screen
- Hiring manager review
- Interviewing
- Offer
- Hired

**Example values:**

| Stage | Candidates |
|---|---:|
| Applied | 142 |
| Recruiter screen | 86 |
| Hiring manager review | 54 |
| Interviewing | 63 |
| Offer | 18 |
| Hired | 6 |

---

### 4. Average time to hire
Show as a KPI card near the top of the screen.

**Component:** KPI card  
**Title:** Average Time to Hire  
**Example value:** `31 days`  
**Optional supporting text:** `-3 days vs last quarter`

---

### 5. Headcount split by department
Show as a donut chart, bar chart, or compact stacked breakdown.

**Component:** Donut chart or horizontal stacked bar  
**Departments to include:**
- Engineering
- Product
- Analytics
- Customer Success
- People
- Other

**Example values:**

| Department | Headcount | Percent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Engineering | 92 | 37% |
| Product | 38 | 15% |
| Analytics | 29 | 12% |
| Customer Success | 41 | 17% |
| People | 22 | 9% |
| Other | 26 | 10% |

---

### 6. Interviews scheduled this week
Show as a weekly agenda or list grouped by day.

**Component:** List or mini schedule  
**Fields per row:**
- Day
- Time
- Candidate
- Role
- Interview type or panel

**Example:**

| Day | Time | Candidate | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 10:00 | Sara M. | Frontend Engineer |
| Mon | 14:30 | Li Chen | Product Manager |
| Tue | 09:30 | Omar A. | Data Analyst |
| Wed | 11:00 | Priya N. | Customer Success Lead |
| Thu | 15:00 | Alex R. | Frontend Engineer |
| Fri | 13:00 | Final panel | Product Manager |

Optional addition: show total interviews this week, for example `18 interviews scheduled`.

---

### 7. Attrition over the past six months
Show as a line chart across six months.

**Component:** Line chart  
**Title:** Attrition — Past 6 Months  
**Metric options:**
- Attrition rate percentage, or
- Number of departures per month

**Example data:**

| Month | Attrition rate |
|---|---:|
| Month 1 | 3.1% |
| Month 2 | 4.0% |
| Month 3 | 3.8% |
| Month 4 | 4.6% |
| Month 5 | 4.2% |
| Month 6 | 5.1% |

You can also show a small KPI summary above or beside the chart:

**Attrition rate last 6 months:** `4.2%`

---

### 8. A way to open a new role
Place a primary action button in the top-right corner of the screen.

**Component:** Primary button  
**Label:** `+ Open a New Role`

When clicked, it should open a modal or side panel with a form.

**Suggested fields:**
- Role title
- Department
- Hiring manager
- Location
- Employment type
- Target start date
- Priority
- Role description / notes

**Example modal:**

```text
Open a New Role
--------------------------------
Role title:        [Text input]
Department:        [Select: Product, Engineering, Analytics, CS, People, Other]
Hiring manager:    [Select or user picker]
Location:          [Text input or select]
Employment type:   [Full-time, Contract, Intern]
Target start date: [Date picker]
Priority:          [High, Medium, Low]

[Cancel]  [Create Role]
```

After submission, the new role should appear in the **Open Roles by Department** section.

---

## Recommended screen structure

| Section | Requirement covered | Suggested component |
|---|---:|---|
| Header | 8 | Page title + primary action |
| KPI row | 1, 4, 7 summary | KPI cards |
| Open roles table | 2 | Data table |
| Candidate pipeline | 3 | Funnel or horizontal bars |
| Department headcount | 5 | Donut chart or stacked bar |
| This week’s interviews | 6 | List or schedule card |
| Attrition trend | 7 | Line chart |
| Button/modal | 8 | Primary button + form modal |

This gives the HR lead one consolidated screen for headcount, hiring demand, pipeline health, interview load, attrition, and the ability to create a new open role.
