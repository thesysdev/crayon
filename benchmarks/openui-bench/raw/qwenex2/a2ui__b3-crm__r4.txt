How each requirement is covered:

1. **Account name & owner** — header with avatar, "Acme Corporation", owner Sarah Chen
2. **Total deal value** — $248,500 KPI card
3. **Current deal stage** — "Negotiation" badge with stage-4-of-6 progress bar
4. **Recent activity in order** — numbered list, newest first
5. **Contacts with roles** — table with name, role (incl. buying-role type), and email
6. **Open follow-ups** — table with task, due date, and owner
7. **Jot a note** — "Jot a note" tab with a textarea and save button
8. **Renewal date + warning** — renewal KPI with "In 24 days" tag plus a warning alert banner
9. **Log a call** — "Log a call" tab with contact picker, date, outcome, and notes

Swap in your real CRM data (names, values, dates) as needed — the structure stays the same.
