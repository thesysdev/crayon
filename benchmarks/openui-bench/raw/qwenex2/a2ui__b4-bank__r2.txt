This single screen covers all 11 requirements:

| # | Requirement | Component Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account balances | **Table** with account names & amounts |
| 2 | Recent transactions | **Table** with merchant, date, amount |
| 3 | Spending by category | **Donut PieChart** with 6 categories |
| 4 | Bills due soon | **Table** with bill name, due date, amount |
| 5 | Vacation savings goal | **Progress** bar at 70% ($4,200 / $6,000) |
| 6 | Credit score | **RadialChart** showing 742 |
| 7 | Move money | **Form** with from/to selects + amount input |
| 8 | Freeze credit line | **AlertDialogBlock** with destructive confirm |
| 9 | Statement period | **Select** dropdown with monthly options |
| 10 | Alert triggers | **SwitchGroup** with 5 toggleable alerts |
| 11 | Dispute a transaction | **DialogBlock** opening a full dispute form |

Items 7–11 are organized into a **Tabs** component to keep the screen scannable while still being fully accessible.
