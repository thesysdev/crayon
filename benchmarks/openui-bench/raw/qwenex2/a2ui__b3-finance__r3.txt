This dashboard covers all seven requirements:

| # | Requirement | Component Used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cash on hand | KPI table row → **$1,240,000** |
| 2 | Monthly burn | KPI table row → **$185,000** |
| 3 | Months of runway | KPI table row → **6.7 months** |
| 4 | Expenses by category | Donut **PieChart** with 6 categories |
| 5 | Transactions > $10k | **Table** with 5 qualifying transactions |
| 6 | Switch months | **Select** dropdown (Jan–Jun 2025) |
| 7 | Download statement | Primary **Button** labeled "Download Statement" |
