| 2 | Recent transactions | Table with date, merchant, category, amount |
| 3 | Spending by category | Donut PieChart (6 categories) |
| 4 | Bills due soon | Table with bill name, due date, amount |
| 5 | Vacation savings goal | Progress bar (68% — $3,400/$5,000) |
| 6 | Credit score | Badge showing 742 + trend note |
| 7 | Transfer money | Form with from/to selects + amount input |
| 8 | Freeze credit line | AlertDialogBlock with confirm/cancel |
| 9 | Statement period | Select dropdown with month options |
| 10 | Alert management | SwitchGroup with 5 toggleable alert types |
| 11 | Dispute a transaction | Form with transaction picker, reason, and details |

The interactive actions (7–11) are organized in a **tabbed interface** to keep the screen scannable while still providing full functionality.
