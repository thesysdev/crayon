3. **Best sellers** — table with units and revenue per item
4. **Out of stock** — table with out-since dates and restock ETAs
5. **On shift** — staff table with roles and hours
6. **Average basket size** — $46.20, shown in the returns card subtitle
7. **Returns this week** — count, value, rate, and top returned item
8. **Promotions** — each running promo with performance and status
9. **Registers** — all five with open/closed status and staffing
10. **Reorder** — a form with item, quantity, and urgency
11. **Switch store** — the store selector at the top
12. **This week vs last week** — grouped bar chart comparison
