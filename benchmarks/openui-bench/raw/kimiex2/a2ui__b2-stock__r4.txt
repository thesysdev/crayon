How each requirement is covered:

1. **Low items + remaining units** — the table lists all 6 flagged items with a "Units left" column (e.g., Wireless Mouse: 4 units) plus a severity status.
2. **Reorder per item** — every row has its own "Reorder" action button in the last column, with a bulk "Reorder all low items" button as a bonus.
3. **Trigger threshold** — stated explicitly in the info line and the alert: the warning fires when stock drops below 20 units.
4. **Search** — a search input above the table filters items by name or SKU.
