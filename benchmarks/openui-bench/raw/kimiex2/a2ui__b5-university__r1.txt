A few notes on how the items map to the screen:

- **Items 2 + 14** share one grouped horizontal bar chart (applications and waitlisted per program) so you can compare demand and backlog side by side.
- **Item 12** (department focus) sits at the top as a selector with Apply/Reset, intended to filter everything below it.
- **Item 15** lives behind an "Approve a new course" dialog button under Actions, so the approval form doesn't clutter the overview.
- **Item 8** is a progress bar since it's inherently a part-to-figure; **item 9** is a donut for the same reason.
